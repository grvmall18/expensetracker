Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect only what is needed to run the service:

  • Account information, such as your name and email address.
  • Expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type, needed to keep the service working.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and improve Spendly: to log you in, store your expenses, and show you summaries of your spending. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database and is accessible only to you through your account. We take reasonable steps to protect it, including storing passwords in hashed form. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses Google Fonts to display text. Your browser may contact Google's servers to load these fonts. We do not share your expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data removed, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
